First product · MVP

Enquiries, organised.

This is a working, browser-based proof of concept for managing sample customer requests. Add records, search, filter, update status and export a CSV file.

Privacy note: this standalone demo stores records in this browser only. It does not send them to a server. Do not enter real or sensitive customer information until a secure backend is built.

Local demo: data is saved in this browser using localStorage, where supported.
